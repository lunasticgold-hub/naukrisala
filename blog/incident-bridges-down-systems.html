Incident bridges: running a call when systems are down

1 min read

When a major system goes down, the incident bridge is the call where responders coordinate. Without discipline, the call becomes twenty people talking at once and nobody fixing anything. The bridge needs a leader who does not fix things personally but keeps everyone else coordinated, a timeline of what happened when, and a single channel for updates to users.

Roles that keep a bridge working

  • Incident lead: runs the call, assigns tasks, and decides when to escalate, without touching the systems directly.
  • Technical lead: owns the diagnosis and the fix, and reports progress in plain language at set intervals.
  • Communications: sends user updates from the timeline, so engineers are not pulled away to answer messages.
  • Note-taker: records every decision and timestamp, which becomes the review material afterwards.

The review after the incident matters as much as the fix. It should produce actions with owners and dates, not blame.
