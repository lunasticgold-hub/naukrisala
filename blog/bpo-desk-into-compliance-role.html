AML & KYC

Moving from a BPO desk into a compliance role

1 min read

People moving from business process outsourcing into compliance often undersell themselves. Verification calls, document checks, case notes, and working to a strict process are exactly the raw material of KYC and monitoring work. The gap is usually vocabulary and context, not ability. Compliance adds judgement about risk to process skills you already have.

Closing the gap in order

  • Learn the core terms first: CDD, EDD, sanctions, transaction monitoring, and suspicious activity reporting, with one real example for each.
  • Practise writing case notes that state facts, evidence, and a decision, since that is the daily output of the role.
  • Follow financial crime news for a month and note how cases were actually caught, which teaches pattern recognition.
  • Prepare interview answers that translate your current tasks into compliance language without exaggerating them.

Apply for analyst roles that list process experience as a requirement, and be ready to explain one difficult case from your current work in detail.
