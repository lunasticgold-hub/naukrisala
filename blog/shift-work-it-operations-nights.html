Shift work in IT operations: staying sharp on nights

1 min read

IT operations runs around the clock, which means night shifts are part of the job, not an exception. The work itself is often quieter at night, but the risk is higher: fewer people are around when something breaks, and tired judgement causes mistakes. People who last in shift work treat recovery as seriously as the shift.

Practices that actually help

  • Protect sleep after a night shift with darkness, quiet, and a fixed wake time, instead of sleeping in irregular bursts.
  • Eat a proper meal before the shift and avoid heavy food in the middle of the night, which worsens the energy dip.
  • Write handovers as if the next person knows nothing, because at night they may genuinely be alone.
  • Use checklists for routine night tasks, since tired brains skip steps that alert brains remember.

If a shift pattern damages your health, raise it with your manager, with specifics. Sustainable rotas are a management responsibility, not a test of toughness.
