ServiceNow certifications: a practical order to attempt them

1 min read

ServiceNow offers a chain of certifications, and the order matters. The Certified System Administrator exam covers platform fundamentals: tables, forms, users, and basic automation. Later exams, such as application development or ITSM implementation, assume that foundation. Skipping ahead usually means relearning basics under exam pressure.

A sensible sequence

  • Begin with system administration, and practise in a personal instance until navigation and configuration feel natural.
  • Next, pick one specialisation that matches the work you want, such as development or ITSM, rather than collecting exams.
  • Study from the official course material and hands-on labs, since exam questions test applied knowledge, not recall.
  • Book the exam only after scoring well on practice tests, and treat a failed attempt as a diagnostic, not a verdict.
