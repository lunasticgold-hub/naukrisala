Finance operations interviews: process questions to expect

1 min read

Finance operations interviews focus on process, not theory. Expect questions like: walk me through invoice processing, or what do you do when a reconciliation breaks. The interviewer wants a sequence with decision points, not a definition. Practise narrating processes end to end until the steps come in order without prompting.

Answering process questions well

  • Start at the trigger: what starts the process, such as receiving an invoice or a bank statement.
  • Walk each step in order, naming the document or system involved at every stage.
  • Pause at control points: where matching, approval, or verification happens, and what happens on failure.
  • End with the outcome: what record exists afterwards, and who can see it.
