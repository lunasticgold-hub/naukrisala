Cloud & DevOps

DevOps interview questions that check systems thinking

1 min read

DevOps interviews rarely ask for tool trivia. They describe a system under stress and ask what you would do: a deployment fails halfway, a service slows down every afternoon, or a disk fills up on a Friday night. The interviewer watches how you narrow the problem, what you check first, and whether you think about users, not just servers.

Answering in a way that scores

  • Start by stating what you would measure before changing anything, since blind changes hide the real cause.
  • Separate symptoms from causes out loud, so the interviewer follows your reasoning.
  • Mention rollback explicitly: every change needs a way back, especially under pressure.
  • End with prevention, such as an alert or a check that would catch the same failure earlier next time.

Practise with real incidents from your own work, even small ones. A true story told clearly beats a textbook answer.
