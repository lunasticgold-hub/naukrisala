Career Change

From customer support to IT: the shortest credible bridge

1 min read

Customer support agents already do IT-adjacent work: troubleshooting, following runbooks, escalating incidents, and explaining fixes. The bridge into IT is technical depth, not a new personality. You need to understand the systems behind the tickets you already handle, and to prove it with practice rather than claims.

The shortest credible bridge

  • Learn how the products you support actually work underneath, starting with the ones you troubleshoot most.
  • Study basic networking and operating systems until you can explain a connectivity problem end to end.
  • Take on technical escalations voluntarily, since they build the record that justifies the move.
  • Certify in one foundational area, chosen to match the IT team you want to join.

Ask your current manager about internal moves early. Internal transfers value your product knowledge, which external hiring cannot see.
