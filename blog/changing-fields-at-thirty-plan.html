Career Change

Changing fields at thirty: a plan that respects your bills

1 min read

Changing fields at thirty differs from changing at twenty because bills, family, and commitments are real constraints, not excuses. The switch still works, but it needs staging: keep income flowing while you learn, move into an adjacent role first, and only then step fully into the new field. Plans that ignore money fail even when the learning succeeds.

Staging the switch

  • Work out the minimum monthly income you must maintain, and treat it as a hard constraint on every plan.
  • Learn while employed, using evenings and weekends, until you have proof of skill in the new field.
  • Target adjacent roles first, where your current experience counts, rather than entry roles that reset everything.
  • Set a decision date for the move, so learning does not drift into a permanent hobby.

Talk to people already in the target field before committing. Their honest account of the work matters more than any course brochure.
