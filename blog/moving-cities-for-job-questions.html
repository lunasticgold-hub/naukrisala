Career Change

Moving cities for a job: costs and questions to settle first

1 min read

Moving cities for a job can transform a career, and it can also strain finances for a year if the costs are underestimated. Rent, deposits, travel home, and higher daily expenses add up, and the first months often bring unexpected spending. Counting the full cost before accepting turns an exciting move into a sustainable one.

Questions to settle before accepting

  • Work out monthly costs in the new city from real listings, not from averages or guesses.
  • Ask about probation terms and notice periods, since early exits are expensive after a move.
  • Check the commute honestly: a long daily commute drains the energy a new job needs.
  • Plan a financial buffer for the first three months, when surprises are most likely.

Visit the city for a few days if possible, including a normal working day. Tourist impressions hide the daily reality of living there.
