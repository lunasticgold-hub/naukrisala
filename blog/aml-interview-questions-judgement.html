AML & KYC

AML interview questions that test judgement, not memory

1 min read

Interviewers for compliance roles rarely ask for textbook definitions. They give you a scenario and ask what you would do. A typical one: a customer with a clean profile suddenly moves large sums through several accounts in a week. The interviewer wants your reasoning, not a single correct answer. Practise talking through scenarios out loud before the interview.

How to answer a scenario well

  • Start by stating what you would check first, such as the account purpose and recent transaction history.
  • Name the red flags in the facts given, and say which ones matter most and why.
  • Explain what would make you escalate versus what would let you close the alert with a note.
  • Mention documentation: what you would record so another analyst could follow your thinking.
