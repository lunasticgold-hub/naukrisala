Testing

Bug reports developers can act on without asking twice

1 min read

Developers fix bugs fastest when the report contains everything needed to reproduce the problem. Reports that say only that something is broken bounce back with questions, and each round trip costs a day. A good report is short, factual, and complete: what you did, what happened, what should have happened, and the evidence.

Parts of a report that gets fixed

  • A title that names the feature and the failure, so the report can be found and triaged at a glance.
  • Steps to reproduce, numbered, starting from a known state, with the exact data used.
  • Expected versus actual behaviour, stated side by side, so the gap is unmistakable.
  • Evidence: screenshots, logs, or recordings attached, with timestamps where they matter.

Check for duplicates before filing. A second report of the same bug wastes the time of everyone who triages it.
