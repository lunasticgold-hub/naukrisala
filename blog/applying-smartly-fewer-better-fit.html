Applying smartly: fewer applications, better fit

1 min read

Mass applying feels productive because the count rises, but recruiters notice fit within seconds. A tailored application shows the match in the first lines: the role, the relevant skill, and one concrete example. Ten such applications produce more interviews than a hundred identical ones, because each one answers the recruiter's first question: why this person for this role.

Tailoring without rewriting everything

  • Read the description and underline the three requirements mentioned most prominently.
  • Reorder your resume bullets so the matching experience appears first for that role.
  • Adjust the opening lines of your application to name the role and one relevant strength.
  • Skip roles where you match nothing: an honest pass saves time for applications that fit.

Track every application with the date and the version sent. Tailoring only works when you remember what you sent where.
