Two offers at once: comparing beyond the headline

1 min read

Holding two offers at once feels like a good problem, and it is, but only if you compare them properly. The first figure mentioned is rarely the whole story. The work itself, the team, the shift pattern, the probation terms, and the growth path matter as much over a year. A careful comparison now prevents regret later.

Comparing like with like

  • Write both offers side by side on the same dimensions: role, team, location, shift, and probation terms.
  • Ask each employer the same follow-up questions, so differences in answers are real, not accidental.
  • Weigh the manager heavily: a good manager in a slightly lesser role beats a poor manager in a better one.
  • Decide by a deadline you set yourself, and decline the other offer promptly and politely.

Never use one offer purely to pressure the other unless you are ready to accept the bluff being called. Honesty keeps both doors open.
