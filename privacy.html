Privacy.

Short version: this site is a static website. There is no account system and no server of ours that stores your data.

What stays on your device

The Apply Assistant keeps your profile in your own browser (local storage) so it can tailor resumes. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Demo form and WhatsApp

The demo form opens WhatsApp with your message. That message goes to WhatsApp under Meta's privacy policy — we receive only what you choose to send.

Jobs board

The board shows live public listings. Apply links take you to the employer's own site, where that employer's privacy policy applies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic visit data (such as pages requested) as part of serving the site. We run no advertising trackers of our own.

Questions

Anything about your data: message us on WhatsApp.
