Placement courses in Bhubaneswar

NaukriSala is 100% online — there are no centres to travel to. If you live in Bhubaneswar, you join the same live online batches as everyone else, from home, in the evening, alongside college or a job. You pay ₹15,000 to start; the rest is paid only after you are placed, in easy installments from your salary.

What you can train for

Five tracks have their own course pages, and there are 15 career tracks in total. The flagship is AML & KYC Analyst — Batch 9 goes live 10 Nov 2026, with free demos from 3 Nov 2026.

  • AML & KYC Analyst — the flagship: 45 days, live online, evening batches.
  • ServiceNow
  • STOM
  • Data Science & AI
  • Full Stack Python
  • All 15 career tracks — including tracks for fresh graduates, career switchers with no coding background, and commerce and finance graduates.

How the fee works

The same fee structure applies wherever you join from:

  • To start: ₹15,000 — one-time onboarding, collected only after your free demo.
  • While you train: ₹0 — nothing more is due during training. Live training, the live jobs board and the Apply Assistant are included.
  • After placement: installments from your salary — the rest is paid only after a verified offer letter. If you are never placed, you pay nothing beyond the ₹15,000 onboarding fee.

Eligibility: graduation in any stream, plus basic English communication. Full fee terms are shared in writing before you enroll — see Fees and How it works.

Jobs from Bhubaneswar

Training is only half the system. The live jobs board lists real openings across India, matched to every NaukriSala track, and every opening links straight to the employer's own career page. Use the board's City filter to see openings listed in Bhubaneswar, or browse Pan-India roles you can apply for from home.
